## Skimming

The article **“Algorithmic Trading Using Machine Learning”** presents a financial trading system based on **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Machine Learning (ML)**. The main objective of the study is to develop a system capable of analyzing historical market data, technical indicators, and financial news in order to make automated buy, sell, or hold decisions.

The study emphasizes the use of **Reinforcement Learning** algorithms, such as **PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization)**, **DQN (Deep Q-Network)**, and **A2C (Advantage Actor-Critic)**, aiming to improve adaptability under different market conditions, including bull, bear, and sideways markets.

The authors conclude that integrating technical indicators with financial news sentiment analysis can improve trading performance and create more intelligent, adaptive, and robust trading systems when compared to traditional approaches based only on technical analysis.

## Scanning

**Article Title:**
*Algorithmic Trading Using Machine Learning*

**Main Topic:**
The application of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in algorithmic trading within financial markets.

**Purpose of the Article:**
To develop and compare reinforcement learning algorithms capable of learning effective trading strategies in financial environments.

**Algorithms Used:**

* PPO (*Proximal Policy Optimization*)
* DQN (*Deep Q-Network*)
* A2C (*Advantage Actor-Critic*)

**Data Sources Used:**

* Historical financial market data
* Technical indicators
* Financial news
* Sentiment analysis

**Problem Addressed:**
Traditional trading systems often lack adaptability and usually ignore subjective market influences, such as the impact of financial news on stock prices.

**Main Results:**
The reinforcement learning algorithms demonstrated the ability to learn profitable trading strategies and adapt to different market conditions.

**Conclusion:**
The integration of multiple data sources with advanced Artificial Intelligence techniques can lead to more efficient, adaptive, and profitable trading systems.

## Technical Glossary
| English Term | Translation | Technical Concept |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Algorithmic Trading** | **Negociação Algorítmica** | Automated trading systems that execute orders based on preprogrammed instructions or machine learning models. |
| **Reinforcement Learning** | **Aprendizado por Reforço** | An area of machine learning where an agent learns to make decisions by performing actions and receiving rewards or penalties. |
| **Sentiment Scores** | **Pontuações de Sentimento** | Numerical values assigned to text (like financial news) indicating whether the context is positive, negative, or neutral. |
| **Technical Indicators** | **Indicadores Técnicos** | Mathematical calculations based on historical price, volume, or open interest of a security to predict market directions. |
| **continuous action spaces** | **Espaços de ação contínuos** | Systems in RL where the available actions are represented by infinite, continuous numerical values rather than discrete choices. |
